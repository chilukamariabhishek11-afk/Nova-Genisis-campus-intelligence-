Mathematical Documentation

Student Success Score & Risk Index Formulation

Comprehensive documentation of indicators, weights, normalization algorithms, and explainability mathematics.

1. Mathematical Model Formulation

The Student Success Score \(S \in [0, 100]\) is synthesized as a constrained linear combination of six normalized sub-scores reflecting academic, behavioral, and placement indicators:

Success Score = 0.30(Academic) + 0.15(Attendance) + 0.15(LMS) + 0.20(Placement) + 0.10(Skills) + 0.10(Feedback & Wellbeing)
🎓 Academic Mastery (30% Weight)

Normalized CGPA scaled to 100, combined with internal test marks, subjected to a strict penalty of -15 points per active backlog.

📅 Attendance Discipline (15% Weight)

Direct overall attendance percentage. If overall attendance drops below the 75% statutory detention threshold, an exponential debarment penalty is applied.

💻 LMS Digital Engagement (15% Weight)

65% assignment completion rate + 35% weekly platform login frequency (capped at 16 logins/week).

💼 Placement Readiness (20% Weight)

35% algorithmic coding benchmark + 35% aptitude test score + 30% behavioral mock interview score.

🛠️ Practical Skills (10% Weight)

60% technical assessments + 40% interpersonal and soft-skill ratings.

🧘 Wellbeing & Campus Life (10% Weight)

60% student wellbeing index + 40% course satisfaction rating to identify mental stress or burnout.

2. Explainable Score Attribution (SHAP-Style Formulation)

Every score is compared against the pre-computed cohort benchmark (\(\mu = 72.8\)). For each indicator dimension \(k\), the individual attribution \(\Delta_k\) is defined as:

Δ_k = W_k × (SubScore_k - BenchmarkMean_k)

This produces clear positive drivers and negative drags without black-box opacity, fulfilling explainability criteria.

5 Executive Slides
Slide 1: Problem Understanding & Campus Context Context

From Fragmented Data Silos to Decision Intelligence

Traditional engineering colleges collect massive amounts of data across disconnected departmental systems (ERP, Biometric Attendance, LMS portals, Placement Cell spreadsheets, and Semester Exam registries). Because these data points remain siloed, student failures, exam detentions, and placement rejections are only caught reactively. Our platform solves this by continuously ingesting all 7 streams into a unified real-time 360° decision engine.

Slide 2: 7-Source Data Ingestion & Synthesis Architecture Architecture

Unified 360° Student Data Model

1. Academic (CGPA, marks, backlogs)
2. Attendance (Overall %, subject attendance)
3. LMS (Logins, assignment completion)
4. Engagement (Hackathons, clubs, certs)
5. Placement (Aptitude, coding, mock interviews)
6. Skills (Tech & soft skill assessments)
7. Feedback (Satisfaction & faculty sentiment)
8. Syllabus (Course & unit progress)
Slide 3: Explainable Scoring & Dual-Risk Identification Scoring & Risks

Transparent Attribution Without Black Boxes

Rather than an opaque classification label, our system generates an explainable Student Success Score (0-100) paired with dual risk flags: Academic Risk (driven by backlogs, low attendance, and failing marks) and Placement Risk (driven by low algorithmic coding scores and interview anxiety). Every score displays exact positive driver points and negative drag factors.

Slide 4: Strategic Student Segmentation & Syllabus Pacing Segmentation & Syllabus

High-Impact Targeted Interventions

We group students into 4 distinct quadrants: (1) High Academics - Low Placement (targets DSA coding bootcamps), (2) Placement Ready - Academic Risk (targets attendance regularization and backlog clearance), (3) Consistent High Achievers (mentorship and marquee placement), and (4) Dual-Risk (urgent 360° faculty counseling). Integrated with real-time syllabus completion tracking to ensure academic delivery stays on schedule.
